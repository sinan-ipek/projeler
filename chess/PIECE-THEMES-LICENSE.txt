Chess piece designs — source and licensing notes (v3.8.1)

Available themes:
- Classic: Cburnett, local SVG assets in chess/pieces/cburnett/
- Chessnut: https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/chessnut
- Tatiana: https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/tatiana

Chessnut and Tatiana are linked as SVG resources from the Lichess repository
at runtime, with a fallback to the local Classic set if loading fails.

Please consult the corresponding upstream artwork authors and licensing
documentation before redistributing, bundling, or modifying SVG assets.
The general license of Lichess code does not automatically establish
the license of every individual piece set.

Prior themed ChessSkins selections were removed in v3.8.1.
